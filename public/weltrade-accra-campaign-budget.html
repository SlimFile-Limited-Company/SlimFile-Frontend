This document outlines the Option B Mid-Range Video Ad Campaign budget for the Weltrade client onboarding program launching in Accra, Ghana. The campaign targets two distinct audiences — young investors (18–35) and high-net-worth professionals & top investors — across five digital platforms over a structured 4-week period. All figures are presented in Ghana Cedis (GHS) with USD equivalents provided for management reference.

Audience Targeting Strategy

🎯 Youth Segment — Ages 18–35

  • Interests: Forex, crypto, side hustles, entrepreneurship
  • Location: Accra metro area (Greater Accra Region)
  • Platforms: TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook, YouTube
  • Message: "Start investing with as little as GHS 500"
  • Hook: Relatable, fast-paced short video content
  • CTA: Register for the Accra program / Free account

💼 Top Investors & Professionals

  • Demographics: Ages 30–55, business owners, finance execs
  • Location: Accra CBD, East Legon, Airport Residential Area
  • Platforms: LinkedIn, YouTube, Facebook
  • Message: "Institutional-grade trading. Partner earnings."
  • Hook: ROI data, broker credibility, partner programme
  • CTA: Book a 1-on-1 consultation / Attend the event
